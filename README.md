# Legal AI Builds

I am William Littman, a technology lawyer and former Head of Legal and Senior Deputy General Counsel at technology and life sciences companies. These are AI workflows I have built for in-house legal work.

My focus is building workflows that are tested, secure, and documented, so they do not leave with the person who built them.

## Builds

| Build | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| [Contract Review Playbook](contract-review-playbook/) | Playbook positions for sales, purchasing, and NDAs, tested against real vendor terms |
| [NDA Review Evaluation Rubric](nda-eval-rubric/) | A 34-criterion rubric that scores AI-generated NDA reviews |
| [AI Vendor Scanner](vendor-scanner/) | A Python tool that finds which AI vendors a codebase calls |
| [Weekly Regulatory Watch](regulatory-watch/) | A scheduled digest of legal developments, each tied to its practical impact |

## How these were built

I build with AI assistance and I am learning Python as I go. My learning log is in `LOG.md`.

All test documents are synthetic, except public vendor terms, which are linked rather than copied.
